About

Hello, my name's Oliver and since April 2025 I have been working as an instrument technician. I trained at Hickies Music shop in Reading for around a year and a half before a shop restructuring led to my role being eliminated, and am now working from home in Woodley, Reading. The services I can provide include, but are not limited to:

  • Thorough cleaning and general maintenance
  • Restringing and setting up for common string instruments
  • Electronic repairs (guitar electronics, basic amplifier and keyboard repairs) and upgrades (new pickups, etcetera)

The instruments I can service include acoustic, electric and classical guitars, violins, violas and keyboards, and limited work on cellos, amplifiers and effect units. Feel free to contact me with any questions about specific repairs, especially if you have a particularly interesting/demanding repair job.

Repairs Pricing

Labour prices are as follows- currently lowered as I start out separately from the shop:

Service Details Starting Cost (labour only)
Restring Includes a full check-over and clean, fret polishing, fretboard conditioning. £20
Setup Neck relief, nut, saddles and bridge are adjusted for the best possible action and intonation, making the guitar feel better to play. Includes a restring as described above. £40
Electronics Silent, buzzing or scratchy jacks, pots, and pickups can be cleaned, fixed, reconnected or replaced as necessary. Usually necessitates a restring as the strings often need to come off to fully access the electronics. £30/hr
Small Repairs Any little problems that need putting right; broken tuners, strap button installation, minor adjustments, single string replacements. These can often be done rather quickly, and are charged according to the problem at hand. Starting at £5

Any necessary parts (strings, etc.) will be charged at cost price to me, including shipping when applicable. The prices above are guides; any substantial labour price increases will be checked with you first before work is carried out.
